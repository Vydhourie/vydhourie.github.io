An Optimization Framework for Focal Node Selection in Interference Detection

Vydhourie Thiyageswaran, Maryam Fazel
Working paper. Related dissertation chapter: Chapter 3, “An Optimization Framework for Focal Node Selection in Interference Detection,” in my PhD dissertation, Leveraging Graph Structure for Optimal Design, Estimation, and Inference under Interference (University of Washington, 2026).

We optimize the selection of focal units for conditional randomization tests of network interference, balancing exposure information and dependence to improve statistical power. Focal assignments are held fixed while auxiliary assignments are rerandomized.

Why selecting more focal units can reduce power
