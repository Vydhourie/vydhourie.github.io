Random Forests for Opponent Hand Estimation in Gin Rummy

Anthony Hein, May Jiang, Vydhourie Thiyageswaran, Michael Guerzhoy

AAAI 2021 · EAAI symposium

Overview

This work studies an agent that combines game-playing heuristics with predictions about an opponent’s hidden hand. Random forests trained on self-play data estimate the probability that each card is held by the opponent. The learned hand model improves performance relative to a baseline heuristic agent.
