Data-Adaptive Exposure Thresholds under Network Interference

Vydhourie Thiyageswaran, Tyler H. McCormick, Jennifer Brennan

NeurIPS 2025 · Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 38

Overview

Exposure mappings determine which observations are used to estimate treatment effects under network interference. We learn exposure thresholds from the observed data by estimating the bias–variance trade-off of a Horvitz–Thompson estimator. Simulations and experiments with synthetic outcomes on real networks compare the method with fixed thresholds and an adapted Lepski procedure.

Related dissertation chapter: Chapter 4. Dissertation on ProQuest.
