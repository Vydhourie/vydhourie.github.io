Optimal Design under Interference, Homophily, and Robustness Trade-offs

Vydhourie Thiyageswaran, Alex Kokot, Jennifer Brennan, Marina Meila, Christina Lee Yu, Maryam Fazel

Preprint · arXiv:2601.17145

Overview

How should experiments be designed when treatment spills across a network and connected units have similar outcomes? We optimize treatment-assignment covariances using a worst-case mean-squared-error bound that balances interference, homophily, and robustness to heterogeneous variation. Semidefinite optimization with Gaussian rounding and an adapted Gram–Schmidt Walk yield implementable designs.
