General Covariance-Based Conditions for Central Limit Theorems with Dependent Triangular Arrays

Arun G. Chandrasekhar, Matthew O. Jackson, Tyler H. McCormick, Vydhourie Thiyageswaran

Preprint · arXiv:2308.12506

Overview

We develop sufficient covariance-based conditions for asymptotic normality in dependent triangular arrays. Affinity sets organize potentially strong dependence without requiring independence outside those sets. The framework accommodates several familiar dependence structures and supports applications including treatment effects with spillovers, covariance estimation, diffusion, and spatial processes.

Related dissertation chapter: Chapter 2. Dissertation on ProQuest.
