Leveraging Graph Structure for Optimal Design, Estimation, and Inference under Interference

Vydhourie R T Thiyageswaran

PhD dissertation · Statistics · University of Washington

Overview

This dissertation uses dependence and network structure to guide inference, testing, estimation, and experimental design. Chapter 2 develops covariance-based central limit conditions using affinity sets. Chapter 3 studies focal-unit selection for interference detection. Chapter 4 learns exposure thresholds from data. Chapter 5 optimizes assignment designs under interference, homophily, and robustness trade-offs.
